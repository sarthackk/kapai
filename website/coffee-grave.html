Is it really invite-only?
Yes. Capacity is limited. Reserving here puts in your request. Kapai confirms your spot over WhatsApp and adds you to the guest list.

No payment on this page. Once you reserve, we confirm on WhatsApp and share payment details, or you settle at the door, your choice.
What's "AM for coffee, PM for ???"
Kapai is your coffee house by day. For one night, after dark, it becomes something else entirely. If you know, you know.
